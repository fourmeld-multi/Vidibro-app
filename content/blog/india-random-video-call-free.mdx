## What people actually talk about

The best conversations we see from Indian users tend to start with specifics, not generals. A few that work:

- **IPL or cricket** — even someone who has never watched cricket knows Virat Kohli. It is a reliable opener with anyone who knows India.
- **Which state are you from** — India is not one place. Someone from Tamil Nadu and someone from Punjab have completely different food, language, and cultural references. That difference is interesting to both sides.
- **Bollywood vs regional cinema** — South Indian cinema has gone global since *RRR* and *Baahubali*. If you are from the south, this is the opening that actually gets a reaction.
- **Chai vs coffee** — genuinely divides people and goes on forever.

What does not work: asking someone their name and city and stopping there. That is not a conversation, it is a form.
